In order to implement gaussian splatting, I first built the inverse covariance matrix using the given rotation and scale vector. I then added the contributions of Gaussians together and reconstructed every pixel by summing together contributions from each Gaussian. For the optimization loop, I ran the forward pass, calculated and saved each current loss, ran the backwards pass, and performed an optimization epoch. For my creative portion, I chose to use a well known Rococo piece called "The Swing" and optimize towards that.
